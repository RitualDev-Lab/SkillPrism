# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are dedicated to building a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community across **SkillPrism** for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Community Standards
- Treat everyone with respect, kindness, and empathy.
- Gracefully accept constructive feedback and diverse perspectives.
- Focus on what is best for the overall developer community.
- Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, offensive commentary, and sharing private data without explicit consent.

## Enforcement & Reporting
If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please reach out to **community@ritualdev.in** or **security@ritualdev.in**. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.
